Entry for the make.fun bounty [Clip GTA 6 for the most YouTube views](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01).

The video is a short vertical clip cut from Rockstar's official Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQRLujxTm3c), the continuous run from 2:16 to 2:44 of the trailer: Jason and Lucia plan in the living room, a swamp helicopter, a jet ski, Jason on his bike under a low plane, a night ride, the boat chase, the two of them in the car, and the sunset highway, closing on the VI and Grand Theft Auto VI logos. The finished clip is 28 seconds, 1080x1920, with the trailer's own audio. It opens on the moment itself, then turns to the viewer: on-screen text reads "Make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun" followed by "make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01", held long enough to read.

The clip was cut and rendered in the agent session that built this repository (ffmpeg: trim, vertical blur-fill crop to 1080x1920, on-screen pitch text), and published as a public YouTube Short from the channel "adu koleksi".

- Video: see `VIDEO.json` for the YouTube Short address.
- Clip file: `clip.mp4` (rendered in this session; kept out of git).
- Program: `main.ts` copies `VIDEO.json` to `/output`. Nothing else.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them in paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.
